# Code of Conduct

Spaceroom is built by people from different countries, languages and backgrounds. We want
taking part to be a good experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Do:
- be welcoming, patient and kind, especially to newcomers and people writing in a second language;
- give and accept constructive feedback about code, not about people;
- assume good intent, and ask before assuming;
- credit other people's work.

Don't:
- harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately;
- make sexualised comments, or comments about someone's identity or appearance;
- publish someone's private information without their permission;
- keep pushing a topic after you've been asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and chat)
and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainers privately. You can use the
**Security › Report a vulnerability** form on the repository for conduct reports too; mark
it "Code of Conduct". Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove
comments, lock threads, or temporarily or permanently ban participants who break this
code.
